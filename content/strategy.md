# Way2Impact page: content strategy (proposal)

Built from the LinkedIn research in `content/research/` (11 profiles the coach chose plus her ~1,700 saved posts). Everything here is a **proposal** for the coach to approve.

## What the research shows works

| Format | Who does it well | Evidence | Fit for a company page |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data drop** on cuts and hiring (grade by grade, with source) | Mai Nazzal | Her top post by far (about 2,600 reactions, 340 reposts) | Excellent: institutional and save-worthy |
| **Before and after rewrite** (one bullet, summary or headline) | Mai Nazzal, Chahrazed Anane, How To Find A Job | Colour-coded CV posts 300 to 600 reactions | Excellent |
| **Vacancy breakdown** ("the job title is not the job") | Nelly Kerubo, Orestes Sotta | Nelly's best 100 to 200 reactions, many reposts | Excellent; strongest sector-specific format |
| **Free resource** (sample CV, playbook, checklist) with keyword comment or first-comment link | Mai Nazzal, Orestes Sotta | Keyword CTA got 288 comments; PDF guides are Orestes' best posts | Good; use a link or landing page (pages cannot easily DM commenters) |
| **Weekly roundup** | Raise for Good, Lena, PurposePhil | Most consistent and most reposted posts | Excellent: the reason to follow |
| **Validation for the silent majority** ("If you applied in August and heard nothing...") plus steps | Mai Nazzal | About 1,000 reactions | Good, in "we" voice |
| **"If I were..." numbered advice** | Eunice Victoria | 150 to 350 reactions | Good |
| **Personal identity and career stories** | Chahrazed Anane, Lena | Their best posts | Keep for the coach's personal profile later |
| **Hard sales posts** (ALL CAPS, pricing only) | Eunice, Mai | 1 to 6 reactions | Avoid |
| **Generic remote-job link lists** | Several saved posts | Low quality, off-sector | Avoid |

Design gap the coach noticed: Nelly and How To Find A Job have weak or inconsistent visuals. Way2Impact's opening is **sector-specific advice plus a clean, consistent brand design**.

## Series structures

**Vacancy breakdown** (Nelly Kerubo model, Way2Impact design)
1. Hook: "[Role] at [Org] is not just [X]. It is [Y]."
2. What the role is really for (the hiring problem).
3. Who it works with.
4. The 5 checks before applying (contract category, nationality, languages, specialisation, stakeholders).
5. "Your CV must show:" 5 to 8 bullets.
6. Deadline and official link. CTA.

**Before and after**
1. Hook naming the segment's pain.
2. The weak line (anonymised, or a typical example).
3. Why it fails (one line).
4. The strong line.
5. The rule behind it (formula: verb + task + scale + result).
6. "Save this" plus a question.

**Data drop**
1. Headline statistic with source.
2. Breakdown (grade, region, sub-sector).
3. What it means for job seekers (3 bullets per segment where relevant).
4. Source line. "Our inbox is open."

## Lead magnets (proposal)

- Sample sector CV per function (MEAL, programme, grants, CSR), colour-coded.
- "5 checks before you apply" one-pager.
- UN application checklist (PHP, motivation statement, CBI).
Delivered via website link (first comment), not DMs.
