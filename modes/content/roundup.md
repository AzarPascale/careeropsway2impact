# Mode: roundup (weekly jobs and consultancy roundup for the Way2Impact page)

Purpose: give people a reason to follow the Way2Impact page every week, and show the coach's expertise through a short tip, which a plain job board can't offer. Built from the Raise for Good, Lena Moral Waldmeier, Nora Marketos (PurposePhil), Zaytoonz and Climate and Remote Opportunities models (`content/research/`). **(proposal: format to be confirmed by the coach)**

**Scope (coach decision, 16 Nov 2026):** jobs and consultancy calls. **No grants.** Global.

## 1. Collect (Monday)

1. Run `modes/scan.md` across `config/portals.yml` for the past 7 days, no client filter.
2. Keep 25 to 50 good roles, then pick **8 to 12** for the post. Balance:
   - levels: Early career / Mid-career / Senior and leadership,
   - at least 2 consultancies or short-term roles,
   - at least 2 remote or home-based roles,
   - at least 1 "great for corporate switchers" role (CSR, ESG, fundraising, finance, ICT, partnerships),
   - regions: mix, no single region over half,
   - employer types: mix UN, INGO, local NGO, foundation, CSR.
3. For each role, open the **official** page and confirm it is open and the deadline is at least 5 days away.
4. Drop anything with a fee, no official source, or vague details.

## 2. Job line format

One line per job:

`Role · Organisation · Remote / Hybrid / On site, City, Country · Grade or salary (if public) · Deadline DD Mon · official link`

Tags you may add: `[Consultancy]`, `[Entry route]`, `[Switcher-friendly]`, `[French]` / `[Arabic]` / `[Spanish]` when a language is required.

## 4. Visual

A branded card every week (text-only weeks perform worse): Way2Impact colours (olive #6B7045, sage #A3AD7F, ochre #C89B4E, warm brown #4A3728, beige #E8DCC4, off-white #FBF8F2), series name, "Week of DD Mon", "N roles · 4 groups". Test a 10 to 15 second video or a 3 to 5 slide carousel version once a month (video roundups did best for Raise for Good).

## 5. Rules

- Every link goes to the official page. No aggregator links in the post.
- Never invent a salary; show the grade for UN posts.
- Tag organisations only when it adds value; tag people only with their consent.
- Save the draft to `content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-roundup.md`. The coach posts it.

## 6. Monthly spin-off (proposal)

"Entry routes this month": YPP, JPO, UNV, paid internships, fellowships (not grants), for segment A.
