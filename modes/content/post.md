# Mode: post (Way2Impact page content)

Drafts posts for the Way2Impact **company page**. Later, the coach's personal profile gets the first-person stories. Inputs: `content/strategy.md` (series and calendar), `content/hooks.md` (hook library), `content/research/` (what works for Mai Nazzal, Chahrazed Anane, Eunice Victoria, Nelly Kerubo, Lena Moral Waldmeier and others).

## Steps

1. Ask (or pick from `content/strategy.md`): which **series**, which **segment** it speaks to (A, B, C, D or all), and the one thing the reader should do after reading.
2. Pick a hook from `content/hooks.md` that fits; write 3 hook options and recommend one.
3. Write the body using the series structure.
4. End with **one** main CTA (save, comment a keyword, link in first comment, free 20-minute call) plus an optional P.S.
5. Suggest the visual: carousel slides (titles per slide), a single card, or text only.
6. Save to `content/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Never post.

## Voice for the page

- **"We"** for Way2Impact; attribute personal notes to the coach by name ("Kristin's note: ...").
- Warm, protective, practical; insider knowledge without sounding superior.
- Short lines, one idea per line, arrows for lists. No em-dashes or en-dashes.
- **Credibility rules:** never claim the coach sat on hiring panels or worked in HR. Her authority is real: years in international and local organisations, NGO management studies, work covering the Middle East and Africa, landing competitive roles, and what clients achieve. Never invent client results; use real, permission-given cases only, anonymised.
- Outrage about named organisations is a brand risk on a page: frame as "what the data shows" or "what candidates tell us", and stay solution-led.
- Cite a source line for every statistic.

## Sector rules for content

- Use sector words correctly (`sector/language-guide.md`); no saviour framing.
- Speak to all four segments over a month, not only senior professionals.
- Give generously: real templates, real rewrites, real data. Sell softly.
