## Update

## Follow-ups (drafts only)

| Situation | When | Draft |
|---|---|---|
| UN application, no news | After about 3 months | Short status enquiry, only if the portal gives a contact |
| INGO or NGO application, no news | 2 to 3 weeks after the deadline | One polite email; none after a second silence |
| After an interview | Within 24 hours | Thank-you note naming one point from the conversation |
| After a rejection | Optional, same week | Thank them, ask to be considered for future posts or the roster |
| Informational interview | Same day | Thank-you plus one follow-up idea |

Sector norm: silence is normal, especially at the UN. A roster placement is an asset; set status `Rostered` and keep watching.
