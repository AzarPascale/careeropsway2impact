# Mode: outreach (networking messages, drafts only)

Uses `sector/research/05` §6. The agent drafts; the client sends from their own account.

## Rules of the sector

- Ask for a **20 to 30 minute conversation to learn**, not for a job. Employers raise openings themselves.
- Start from a **shared link**: alumni, a mutual contact, same duty station, same cluster, a post they wrote.
- Look beyond HR and big brands: people in the target role, partners, grantees, consultants who work with the team.
- Ask each contact who else to speak to. Thank them the same day.
- **One** polite follow-up after about a week; nothing after a second silence.
- **Never** contact panel members during an open selection, and do not ask contacts to forward a CV for a live UN post unless they offer. Referrals do not bypass UN screening.
- Some organisations do not do informational interviews (for example the Gates Foundation, per research/03). Respect it.

## Drafts

1. **LinkedIn connection note** (under 300 characters): the shared link, one specific reason, a light ask.
2. **Informational interview request** (email or message, under 120 words): who the client is in one line, why this person, 2 to 3 focused questions they want to ask, 20 minutes at the contact's convenience.
3. **Question list** for the call (5 to 7): path into the role, what the team values, how hiring really works there, skills that matter now, who else to talk to.
4. **Thank-you** (same day) and **one follow-up** (after a week, only if no reply).

Save to `clients/<code>/output/outreach-<org>.md`.
