# 07. Course Catalogue (Tiered: Free, Affordable, Premium)

For: the Way2Impact AI job-search system (gap analysis module)
Compiled: 16 November 2026
Method: WebSearch (standard mode) and WebFetch against official provider pages where possible. Third-party listings were only used when the official page could not be reached, and these are marked "Partial".

---

## Part 1. How to use this catalogue (rules for the AI)

1. **Always give three options per skill gap: one Free, one Affordable, one Premium.** If a tier has no verified option for that skill, say so plainly ("No verified affordable option; nearest alternative is ...") rather than inventing one.
2. **Certificate first.** Within each tier, prefer a course that gives a certificate, badge or exam-based certification. Only suggest a no-certificate option when nothing with a certificate exists, or when the learning value is clearly higher (for example a core standard like Sphere).
3. **Match the gap and the target role.** Tie each suggestion to the specific missing requirement in the job ad or role profile (for example "ad asks for MEAL system design and KoBo" leads to MEAL DPro plus KoboToolbox Essentials, not a generic data course).
4. **Prefer sector-recognised credentials.** Order of preference: (a) credentials named in job ads or mandatory for the employer type (BSAFE for UN, PSEA for UN partners, Project DPro for INGO project roles), (b) courses from the standard-setting body (Sphere, CHS Alliance, CALP, Alliance CPHA, IPC, GBV AoR), (c) recognised university or professional bodies, (d) generic MOOCs.
5. **Match the employer type.**
   - UN agency targets: BSAFE, UN CC:Learn, UNITAR, UNSSC, I Know Gender, Agora, OpenWHO.
   - INGO / humanitarian targets: Kaya, DisasterReady, PM4NGOs, CALP, Sphere, CHS, HEAT.
   - Donor / compliance roles: Humentum, EU Academy (DG ECHO), Bond.
   - CSR / ESG / social enterprise targets: GRI, SROI, UN Global Compact Academy, B Lab, Acumen.
6. **State language availability and time needed** for every suggestion. For French, Spanish and Arabic speakers, prefer courses confirmed in that language (Kaya, DisasterReady, Sphere, CHS, CALP Fundamentals, BSAFE and many UN courses have multilingual versions).
7. **State cost honestly**, including hidden costs: some "free" platforms now charge for certificates (UN CC:Learn says USD 10 to 30 per certificate is being rolled out; edX and FutureLearn charge for certificates; Coursera charges unless financial aid is approved).
8. **Use tiered pricing to the client's advantage.** PM4NGOs DPro exams cost USD 30, 85 or 180 depending on the candidate's status. RedR UK HEAT has lower bands for national NGO staff and self-funders. Tell the client which band they likely qualify for, but never tell them to claim a band they do not qualify for.
9. **Coursera financial aid:** for Coursera certificates, tell clients they can apply for financial aid per course (decision takes roughly two weeks; not available for Coursera Plus).
10. **Check the "Verified" column.** If it says "Partial" or "No", add a short line telling the client to confirm the price and certificate on the provider page before enrolling. Never present an unverified price as fact.
11. **Do not recommend** courses marked "Status unclear" unless the client has confirmed they still exist.
12. **Budget fit:** Affordable means roughly under USD 300 all-in. Premium means higher cost and usually a recognised credential. Many premium items are often paid by employers (HEAT, SSAFE, PRINCE2, PMP); say so, because clients in a job search may be able to defer them.

### Verified column legend

- **Yes 11/2026**: existence and key details (cost and/or certificate) confirmed on the official provider page or official listing during this research (November 2026).
- **Partial 11/2026**: course confirmed to exist, but price, certificate or dates come from third-party sources, are conflicting, or were not shown on the official page.
- **No**: not checked in this session; based on prior knowledge only. Must be checked before recommending.
- **Status unclear**: evidence suggests the course or provider may have changed or closed.

|---|---|---|---|
