## 2. Structure

### Inspira and agency portals
These are forms, not free layouts. **(General practice, not in the research: check on the live portal.)** The client fills fields such as education, work history and languages, then answers questions for the job. The structure is set by the form. The client controls the words inside each field.

What the research confirms:
- **Pre-screening questions** are common. The application is checked against the minimum requirements first (research/03 §2).
- The job opening lists the **competencies** the client will be scored on (research/03 §3.1).
- UNDP runs a pre-recorded video interview and/or a written test in phase 1 (research/03 §3.2). The CV gets the client to that point. It does not need to tell every story.

### The P11
A form with set fields. Fill every field. Do not attach a CV in place of a P11 when the advert asks for a P11.

**What a P11 usually asks (general practice, not in the research: versions differ between agencies, and many now use an online profile instead. Check the version the advert links to.)**
- Personal details that a free CV leaves out, such as date of birth and nationality. On a P11, fill the fields the form asks for. The "leave personal details off" rule in the other format files does not apply to a form that asks for them.
- Education, with dates, degrees and main fields of study.
- Languages, often rated separately for reading, writing, speaking and understanding.
- Employment record, starting with the current or most recent post. Each entry can ask for exact dates, title, employer, supervisor, the number and kind of staff supervised, salary, the reason for leaving, and a description of duties.
- References from people who are not relatives.
- A signed declaration that the information is true.

What this means for the client:
- **The P11 is signed as true.** Every date, title and salary must match the master CV, the LinkedIn profile and what referees will say (research/03 §2).
- **The duties field is where results go.** Write it like the CV bullets: results in the job opening's wording, not a list of tasks.
- **The "reason for leaving" field needs the same neutral line as the CV.** For segment D, use the gap wording in `cv-variant-strategy.md` §4 D.
- You fill the personal fields with the client, in the real form. Never paste a filled P11 into Claude (`claude-prompts.md`).

## 3. Length

There is no page limit in a form. The limit is relevance.
- Fill every field the form asks for. An empty required field can fail screening (research/03 §2).
- Inside each work-history field, keep 3-6 bullets that match the job opening. Proposal; adjust once you see real forms.
- For an uploaded CV on an agency portal, follow the advert. If the advert says nothing, use the NGO two-page layout (`ngo-two-page.md`).

## 4. Must-have content

| Section | What it must show | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Work history with exact dates | Every role, start and end month, contract type, duty station | Screening checks experience against the minimum years (research/03 §2) |
| Results per role | 3-6 bullets in the job opening's competency wording | Each opening names its competencies (research/03 §3.1) |
| Education | Degrees with field and year | YPP requires at least a first degree in the field (research/03 §3.5) |
| Languages | Each language with an honest level | Language is tested in UN assessments (research/03 §3.2). YPP requires fluent English or French (research/03 §3.5) |
| Answers to pre-screening and job questions | Short, direct, one example each | Pre-screening is part of screening (research/03 §2) |
| References | Ready, but only where the form asks | UNDP checks references for candidates not already UN staff (research/03 §3.2) |

## 5. Language and wording

- **Mirror the job opening.** Use the words of its "Competencies" section in the bullets (research/03 §3.1).
- **Know the framework.** The UN Values and Behaviours Framework has 4 values (Inclusion, Integrity, Humility, Humanity) and 5 behaviours (Connect and Collaborate; Analyse and Plan; Deliver Results with Positive Impact; Learn and Develop; Adapt and Innovate) (research/03 §3.3). Some postings may still use the older competency list (research/03 §3.3, secondary source). Use whichever list the opening uses.
- **Results, not duties.** "Delivered X for Y, which led to Z." The competency interview later asks for the full story as Situation, Action, Result (research/03 §3.3). The CV bullet is the headline of that story.
- **Plain English or French.** Application language follows the advert.
- **AI:** fine for proofreading, structuring and reading the job description. Not fine for inventing or inflating experience, or for pasting generic answers (UNDP rules, research/03 §3.7).

## 6. Common mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pasting a corporate CV into the form | Rewrite each field in the opening's competency wording |
| One generic profile for every opening | Keep the profile complete, then tailor the job-specific answers and bullets each time |
| Dates that do not match the CV, LinkedIn or P11 | Copy every date from the master CV |
| Duties without results | One result per bullet, with a number where true |
| Skipping or rushing pre-screening questions | Answer each one directly. They are part of screening (research/03 §2) |
| Overstating a language level | Give the honest level. Languages are tested (research/03 §3.2) |
| Generic AI-written answers | Adapt every answer to the client's own experience (research/03 §3.7) |
| Treating a rejection as final | Qualified runners-up can go on a roster (research/03 §3.4). Keep the profile current |

## 7. Annotated skeleton (for the work-history fields and an uploaded UN-style CV)

Curly braces `{ }` are fields to fill. Lines starting with `>>` are notes for you or the client. Delete them before sending.

```
{FULL NAME}
{City, Country} · {email} · {phone}
>> Use only the personal details the form or advert asks for.

PROFILE (2-3 lines; only if the format allows free text)
{Role family} professional with {n} years in {technical area} in {region}.
{One result that matches the opening's top competency}.
>> Mirror the job title and 1-2 key requirements from the opening.

WORK EXPERIENCE (reverse date order)
{Job title} · {Employer} · {Duty station} · {Month Year} to {Month Year}
{Contract type: staff / consultant / intern / volunteer}
- {Action verb} {what} {with or for whom} {scale}, {result}.
  >> Use the competency words from the opening, not the framework labels.
- {Second result bullet}
- {Third result bullet}
>> 3-6 bullets per relevant role. 1 line for unrelated roles.

{Career transition entry, if any}
>> See cv-variant-strategy.md §4 D for the one-line gap entry.

EDUCATION
{Degree}, {Field} · {University}, {Country} · {Year}
>> Field matters for YPP eligibility (research/03 §3.5).

LANGUAGES
{Language}: {level} {certificate and year, if any}
>> Honest levels only. Languages are tested (research/03 §3.2).

TRAINING AND CERTIFICATES
{Course or certificate} · {Provider} · {Year}

SKILLS
{Tools, including AI and data tools the client really uses}
```
