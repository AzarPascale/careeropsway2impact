**This is a client-facing template. The form text speaks to the client as "you".**

## For Kristin: how to use this file

- **Build it as a Google Form.** One Google Form section per section below. Mark questions "required" only where the form says *(required)*. Everything else is optional.
- **When to send it:** in the welcome email right after sign-up and payment, with the request for documents and the Week 0 booking link (`program/gap-analysis-framework.md` §3). The client returns it at least [INTAKE-LEAD-DAYS] days before the Week 0 call.
- **Languages:** make one form per language: English [INTAKE-FORM-LINK], French [INTAKE-FORM-LINK-FR], Arabic [INTAKE-FORM-LINK-AR]. Translate the wording, keep the structure.
- **File uploads:** a Google Forms file-upload question needs the client to sign in to Google. If a client has no Google account, ask them to send the CV and adverts by email or on [MESSAGING-CHANNEL] instead. The form below offers both.
- **Read section 8 ("How you are doing") first,** before you open the CV (`program/gap-analysis-framework.md` §9).
- **Other offers:** for a single-service gap analysis report, use the whole form. For other single services you do not need this form. (The Lighter packages are paused since 29 October 2026.)
- **Time to fill:** about 30-40 minutes (estimate; check it with your first clients).

### Welcome

Thank you for joining. This form helps me prepare your gap analysis. I read every answer before our Week 0 call, so the call can be about you, not about filling in details.

- It takes about 30-40 minutes. You can save time by having your CV and 3-5 job adverts ready.
- Answer in English, French or Arabic, whichever is easiest for you.
- Only questions marked *(required)* must be answered. You can skip anything else. If a question feels too personal, skip it. We can talk about it on the call, or not at all.
- What you write here is confidential. Section 10 explains how I use and store it.
- Please send it back by **{date}**, at least [INTAKE-LEAD-DAYS] days before our Week 0 call.

### Section 3. Your background

17. Your other jobs, internships and volunteering, most recent first. A short list is fine. Your CV can do the rest.
18. How many years have you worked in total? How many of those in NGOs, the UN, donors, embassies, foundations or CSR?
19. Have you ever done any of the following, in a job, studies, volunteering, a family business or a student group? Tick all that apply.

    - Written a report for a donor or a manager
    - Worked with partners, communities or government
    - Written a proposal, concept note or funding request
    - Collected or analysed data
    - Led or trained other people
    - Organised an event or a project from start to finish

20. List 3 things you achieved that you are proud of. A result or a number helps, but it is not required.
21. Certificates or courses you have finished (with the year).
22. Is there anything in your career you worry about explaining? For example a gap, a short job or a contract that ended early.
    *Why I ask: almost everyone has something. Knowing it early lets us plan how to present it well.*
