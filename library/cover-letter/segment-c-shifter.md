## The fictional advert (short version, translated from Arabic)

**Senior Community Responsibility Specialist** (أخصائي أول للمسؤولية المجتمعية), [COMPANY-C], head office, [CITY-C]. Ref. [JOB-REF-C].

[COMPANY-C] is a fictional retail group in the Gulf. Its published sustainability report names youth employment as the focus of its community programme.

Requirements:
1. A bachelor's degree in business, social sciences or a related field.
2. At least 5 years of corporate experience, for example in marketing, communications or project management.
3. Experience managing budgets and partnerships with non-profit organisations.
4. Ability to measure and report the impact of community programmes.
5. Experience engaging employees in volunteering.
6. Fluent Arabic and English. Knowledge of GRI reporting is an asset.

### What each paragraph says, in English

This is a summary to help you follow the letter. It is not a version to send.

| Paragraph | Job | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why them | "I read in your latest sustainability report that youth employment is the focus of your community programme, and that you measure its impact every year. That is what I am looking for: community work tied to the business, with clear results." |
| 2 | Why this role | "I have worked for eight years in marketing and project management at [BANK-C]. In the last two years, community work has become a fixed part of my job and my own time. This role is the natural next step: putting my business experience to work for social impact, full time." |
| 3 | Proof | Four points, one per requirement: a USD 400,000 yearly sponsorship budget, a third of it moved into partnerships with three youth-training NGOs; five indicators and a quarterly report (210 of 300 trainees completed); a volunteering programme with 300 staff in year one; a board seat at [NGO-C] and the GRI certificate in progress. |
| 4 | The ask | Location, fluent Arabic and English (requirement 6), notice period, a request to meet, thanks. |

## Why this works

> **The corporate past is the selling point.** For shifters, commercial, finance and partnership skills are in demand, and the corporate background is an asset, not a gap (research/01 §8.2). The letter leads with budgets, reporting and staff engagement, which a company understands.
>
> **The mission gap is closed with facts.** The research suggests board seats, volunteering or a short certificate to close the "mission credibility" gap (research/01 §8.2). The letter shows a board seat and a certificate. The certificate is honestly marked as in progress. The GRI Certified Sustainability Professional exam is named in research/03 §8 as a credential that helps positioning.
>
> **Business tone, social result.** Each proof point links money or staff time to a measurable result for young people. That is the CSR link between impact and the business (framework §4).
>
> **The advert's own words.** "Partnerships", "measure and report", "volunteering" come straight from the advert, in the same order. CSR hiring often uses an applicant tracking system first, so plain matching words matter (research/03 §8, secondary sources).
>
> **Arabic done simply.** One greeting line, then straight to the point. Modern Standard Arabic, short sentences, one digit style (1 2 3) all through. First-person past verbs (أدرتُ، وضعتُ، أطلقتُ) do not change with gender, so there are few agreement traps. Check any adjective about yourself.

## Make it yours

- Replace every fact with a true fact from your master CV (Week 2). If you have not started a certificate, do not mention it.
- Change paragraph 1 to something real from the company's own report or website.
- If the advert is in English, write in English. Do not translate this letter line by line. Rebuild it from the framework.
- Set the paragraph direction to right-to-left before you export to PDF. Check that Latin text such as "GRI" sits in the right place (framework §6).
