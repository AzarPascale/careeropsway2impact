# Example letter, Segment A: New grad breaking in

**Client-facing.** This page speaks to you, the client, as "you". You can read it in Week 2 of the programme.
**FICTIONAL EXAMPLE.** The employer, the job, the candidate and every fact in this letter are invented to show the method. Do not copy the facts. Copy the structure.

## The fictional advert (short version)

**Programme Assistant, Education in Emergencies**, [ORGANISATION-A], Beirut office. Ref. [JOB-REF-A]. 12-month contract.

[ORGANISATION-A] is a fictional international NGO. It runs non-formal education for out-of-school children through local partner organisations.

Requirements:
1. A bachelor's degree in social sciences, education or a related field.
2. Up to 2 years of experience, including internships or volunteering.
3. Data entry and simple reporting skills (Excel).
4. Ability to work respectfully with children, families and partner staff.
5. Fluent English and Arabic. French is an asset.

## The letter

> [CANDIDATE-A-NAME]
> Beirut, Lebanon · [CANDIDATE-A-EMAIL] · [CANDIDATE-A-PHONE]
>
> [DATE]
>
> Hiring Team, [ORGANISATION-A], Beirut
>
> **Subject: Programme Assistant, Education in Emergencies (Ref. [JOB-REF-A])**
>
> Dear Hiring Team,
>
> [ORGANISATION-A] works through local partners to bring out-of-school children back into learning. I believe that is the right way to work, because partners stay after projects end. I saw this up close during my internship, and I want to start my career in an organisation that works this way.
>
> The Programme Assistant role is the right first step for me. I want to learn the full programme cycle from the inside, and I can bring solid data skills, field experience with children and families, and three languages from day one.
>
> I meet the requirements of the role:
>
> - **Data and reporting.** During a four-month internship at [LOCAL-NGO-A], I entered and cleaned attendance data for 240 learners across 6 learning centres. I built a simple Excel summary sheet. The monthly attendance report then took half a day instead of two days.
> - **Working with children, families and partners.** For one year I volunteered every week as a tutor at a community centre. When attendance dropped, I called each family with the centre coordinator, and 9 of 14 children came back within a month.
> - **Education focus.** My bachelor's in sociology ended with a thesis on why displaced children leave school. I interviewed 20 parents in Arabic and wrote the findings in English.
> - **Languages.** I work in Arabic and English, and I use French in reading and writing.
>
> I am based in Beirut, I can start on [START-DATE], and I have the right to work in Lebanon. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can support your education team. Thank you for your time.
>
> Kind regards,
> [CANDIDATE-A-NAME]

## Why this works

> **Why them, not anyone.** The first paragraph names one specific thing about the employer (working through local partners) and says why it matters to the candidate. It does not praise "your important mission".
>
> **Little paid experience, still strong proof.** A new grad does not have years of jobs. This letter uses an internship, volunteering and a thesis. Each point has a result with a number.
>
> **The advert's words.** "Data entry", "reporting", "partners", "families" are the advert's own words. A screener can tick each requirement quickly.
>
> **Honest about the gap.** French is "an asset" in the advert. The letter says exactly what level the candidate has, no more.
>
> **A clear ask.** Location, start date and right to work are all in the last paragraph.

## Make it yours

- Replace every fact with a true fact from your master CV (Week 2).
- Change paragraph 1 to something real about the employer you are applying to.
- For a UN Volunteers or internship application, check the portal first. It may ask questions instead of a letter. Use the same logic in each answer.
