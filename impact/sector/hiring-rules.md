# Hiring rules: quick reference

The short version of how hiring works in the sector. Full evidence, with a source after each rule: `impact/sector/research/05-career-ops-sector-rules-web-research.md` (web research), `impact/sector/research/03-hiring-processes-and-tests-in-the-sector.md` (processes and tests), `impact/sector/research/01-funding-cuts-and-job-market-2025-2026.md` (market), `impact/sector/primer.md` (system, glossary, context).

## 1. CVs (research/05 §1)
- 2 pages for NGO and INGO; 3 for senior staff and consultants (coach decision, 17 Nov 2026). UN forms have no page limit.
- UN: the online form (PHP or P11) is the application; every month accounted for; every nationality listed; unfinished degrees shown as ongoing; languages rated honestly.
- Results with scale: budget, donor, partners, people reached, geography, languages, efficiency.
- No photo for UN, INGO, donor, UK, US, Canada. Optional in Germany. Gulf and Middle East corporate and CSR roles: photo permitted and often expected; include a professional headshot unless the ad says otherwise (coach decision, 17 Nov 2026; details in `impact/modes/cv.md` §2).
- Mirror the ad's exact terms and competency names, only where true.
- Detect the portal from the apply link and produce the right output (`impact/sector/employer-types.md`).

## 2. Letters and AI rules (research/05 §2)
- One page or less; follow every portal limit. Inspira motivation statement reportedly 2,000 characters **(verify on the live form)**.
- UK charities: supporting statement against the person specification.
- **AI rules differ by employer.** UNDP allows AI for proofreading; some charities and civil society groups (for example CIVICUS) ban AI-written applications. Check the ad and the employer site; tell the coach before drafting; switch to coaching when AI writing is banned.
- Check eligibility before writing.

## 3. Process (research/05 §4)
- UN 3 to 9 months from deadline to offer; silence is normal; apply before the last day.
- Rosters are an asset, not a queue. Roster validity is **(uncertain)**: 1 year vs 2 to 3 years.
- References early; misconduct disclosure; police certificate.
- Fee requests are scams.

## 4. Interviews (research/05 §4.2, research/03 §3)
- Competency-based, against a named framework. One real example per answer, own role clear, humility, context knowledge, localisation awareness, safeguarding values.

## 5. Pay (research/05 §5)
- UN grade fixed; step, start date, relocation are the levers; compare net pay at the duty station.
- INGO bands; national vs international scales.
- Consultants within published fee bands.

## 6. Networking (research/05 §6)
- Learn, don't ask for jobs. One follow-up. Never contact a panel during a live selection.

## 7. Market and job security (research/05 §7, primer §5)
- ReliefWeb vacancies down about 65% from 2024 to 2025 (ALNAP SOHS 2026). Local NGOs and young professionals hit hardest.
- Jesús Baena's ReliefWeb dashboard (about 47,000 postings classified): postings about 57% below peak; consultancies hold at about 1 in 5 postings; more jobs in capitals than in the field; field jobs mostly tied to grants (`impact/content/research/profiles-batch-b.md`).
- Growing: fundraising and partnerships, ICT and data, compliance. Shrinking: global health, chief of party, some M&E (Devex data, snippets).
