# Employer types: format, process and what wins

The agent identifies the employer type in every evaluation (Block A) and uses this table for format, tone and prep. Detail and sources: `impact/sector/research/03` (hiring processes and tests), `impact/sector/research/05` §1 to §6, and `impact/library/`.

| | UN system | INGOs and federations | Local and national NGOs | Donors, embassies, development agencies | Foundations | CSR, ESG, corporate foundations | Consultancies, contractors, social enterprises |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Application** | Online form (Inspira PHP, agency portal, P11). The form is the record; attached CV may not be read | 2-page CV + tailored letter via portal (Workday, Oracle, own system) | CV + letter, often by email; sometimes local-language form | Form or anonymised CV (FCDO), EU: Europass, embassy local forms | CV + letter; letter is a writing sample | Corporate ATS CV, one column | CV as bid annex, EOI, technical note, daily rate |
| **CV format** | `impact/library/cv-formats/un-inspira-and-p11.md` | `impact/library/cv-formats/ngo-two-page.md` | NGO two-page, local norms | `europass.md` for EU; anonymised for UK | NGO two-page | `corporate-csr.md` | Topical or functional by expertise; donor CV formats in tenders |
| **Letter** | Motivation statement (reported 2,000-character limit on Inspira, verify), mapped to competencies | One page, mandate + 3 requirement-to-evidence paragraphs | Short, context and language | UK: supporting statement against person specification, one heading per criterion | Mission-specific, polished | Business case + impact | Cover email + methodology |
| **First screen** | Yes/no eligibility questions, then human review against competencies | HR screen on essential criteria, languages | Hiring manager directly | Anonymised scoring against criteria | Small team, network matters | ATS keywords then HR | Bid manager, speed |
| **Tests** | Written test (technical, analytical), sometimes timed online | Technical test or written task (proposal section, budget, MEAL plan, sitrep) | Sometimes a task | Written tests, sometimes language tests | Writing task | Case or presentation | Proposal is the test |
| **Interview** | Competency-based (CBI) panel against UN values and behaviours framework | Panel: HR + technical + manager; competencies; safeguarding questions | Informal to panel; context knowledge | Structured competency panel; security vetting later | Values and fit | Behavioural, business impact | Fast call, references |
| **Checks** | References (often 3), misconduct screening (ClearCheck), medical, police certificate | References, Misconduct Disclosure Scheme, safeguarding | References | Security clearance, references | References | Background check | References from past clients |
| **Pay** | Grade fixed by post; step, post adjustment, relocation | Pay bands; national vs international scales | Local scale, limited room | Fixed scales | Bands | Corporate negotiation norms | Daily rate within bands |
| **Timeline** | 3 to 9 months | 1 to 3 months | Weeks | 2 to 6 months | 1 to 3 months | Weeks to 2 months | Days to weeks |
| **What wins** | Exact match to each stated requirement and competency; every month accounted for; right grade | Donor and field evidence; scale; languages; safeguarding awareness | Context, language, network, availability | Precise evidence against each criterion | Writing quality and mission depth | Business results translated into impact | Directly relevant past assignments, availability, rate |

## Detecting the employer's application system

| Apply link contains | System | Output to produce |
|---|---|---|
| `careers.un.org`, `inspira` | UN Inspira | Form field text + motivation statement (`impact/modes/un-form.md`) |
| `myworkdayjobs` | Workday | One-column plain CV, no tables or graphics |
| `oraclecloud`, `taleo` | Oracle / Taleo | Same as Workday |
| `jobs.giz.de`, `eu-careers`, `epso` | GIZ / EU | Europass or institution format |
| Email address only | None | CV PDF + letter in email body |

## Rules for the agent

- Always state the employer type and the system in Block A of the evaluation.
- Use the advert's own instructions over this table when they differ.
- For mixed types (for example a UN agency hiring a consultant through UNOPS), use the contract type for pay and process, and the agency for format and competencies.
