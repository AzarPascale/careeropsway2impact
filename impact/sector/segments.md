# The four client segments

From the Way2Impact positioning. The agent reads `config/impact.yml` → `segment` and applies these rules everywhere: scoring (`impact/config/scoring.yml`), CV strategy, letter angle, interview prep, gap analysis and content.

## A. New grads (breaking in)

- **Situation:** degree, maybe a master's; internships, volunteering, student projects; little or no paid sector work. Entry posts are scarce: young professionals were among the hardest hit by the 2025 cuts (ALNAP SOHS 2026, in `impact/sector/research/05` §7).
- **Targets:** UNV (national and international), UN internships (paid where possible), NO-A / NO-1 posts if national, YPP (age 32 or under), JPO (if the client's country funds them), fellowships, local and national NGOs, INGO assistant and officer roles, research assistants, consultancies on data and research.
- **CV angle:** education and relevant coursework near the top; volunteering and internships written as real experience with scale; languages and field exposure prominent; one page of strong content beats two thin pages.
- **Letter angle:** a specific link between studies or volunteering and the programme; eagerness shown through evidence, not adjectives.
- **Scoring:** skip threshold 3.5. Boost entry routes.
- **Gap analysis:** usually certificates that ads ask for (PMD Pro, MEAL basics, safeguarding/PSEA, HEAT or BSAFE for field posts), plus one practical skill (KoBoToolbox, Excel, Power BI).

## B. Moving up

- **Situation:** 2 to 8 years in the sector; stuck at officer level, or wants INGO to UN, or field to regional/HQ.
- **Targets:** one grade up. P-2/P-3 from INGO officer; P-3/P-4 from INGO manager; coordinator and manager roles; country office to regional.
- **CV angle:** scope and complexity (budget, donors, team, partners, locations); strategic contribution; government and donor engagement. Do not assume INGO years speak for themselves: translate them into the competencies of the target post.
- **Scoring:** flag anything more than one grade up.
- **Gap analysis:** management and leadership credentials, donor-specific compliance, a specialisation (cash, protection, MEAL, grants), languages.

## C. Shifters (corporate to impact)

- **Situation:** strong corporate career (finance, marketing, HR, IT, consulting, operations); wants meaning; speaks corporate.
- **Targets (best odds):** CSR and ESG, corporate foundations, development banks, fundraising and partnerships, ICT and data, finance and grants, operations, compliance, social enterprises, consultancies. Devex data shows fundraising and ICT roles grew in 2025 while programme roles shrank (`impact/sector/research/05` §7).
- **CV angle:** translate every result (see `impact/sector/language-guide.md` §1); keep numbers; add any volunteering, board or pro bono work; show cultural adaptability and languages.
- **Letter angle:** why now and why this organisation, without "giving back" clichés; what transfers; humility about what they will learn. Do not present the move as a favour to the sector.
- **Pay:** expect lower pay; answer calmly if asked.
- **Scoring:** add the translation check.
- **Gap analysis:** a sector foundation credential (humanitarian essentials, MEAL, project management for development), plus safeguarding.

## D. Repositioning (cut in the funding crisis)

- **Situation:** experienced, role ended by US or other donor cuts or UN80; possibly in a shrinking sub-sector; morale may be low (the coach handles emotional support, never the agent).
- **Targets:** adjacent sub-sectors and functions that grew; multi-donor or core-funded employers; development banks, EU, Gulf donors, foundations; consultancies as bridges; national posts where eligible.
- **CV angle:** strongest transferable results first; redundancy explained in one plain line ("Position ended when the USAID-funded programme closed in March 2025."); recent learning shown.
- **Interview angle:** short, factual answer on the gap; what they did during it; why this role, not "any role".
- **Scoring:** standard weights (funding security stays at 15%; always comment on funding risk in the report). Consultancies accepted as bridges.
- **Gap analysis:** skills in growing areas (fundraising from non-traditional donors, cash, anticipatory action, climate, data and AI, compliance).
