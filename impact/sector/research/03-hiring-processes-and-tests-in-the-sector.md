# Hiring processes and tests in the social-impact sector

**Date:** 4 October 2026
**Prepared for:** Kristin's career coaching practice (interview and test prep, job tracker)
**Method:** Web research on official employer pages, job boards and pricing pages. Every number or claim has its URL next to it. Anything not confirmed on a primary page is marked **(unverified)** or **(secondary source)**.

---

## 1. Key findings

1. Almost every employer in this sector uses the same 5 steps: **screen the application, test, interview on competencies, check references and safeguarding, then offer.** The tests and the names of the competencies change. The shape does not. (UN: https://careers.un.org/assessment · NRC: https://www.nrc.no/career/five-steps-of-nrc-recruitment-process · US State Dept guide to international organisations: https://iocareers.state.gov/Content/Public/process)
2. **The competency-based interview (CBI) is the core skill.** The UN, UNDP, UNICEF, the UK FCDO, NRC and IRC all score past behaviour. They want one real example per question, told as Situation, Action, Result (UN) or STAR (UK). (https://hr.un.org/en/cbi-training-applicants · https://www.undp.org/careers/our-recruitment-process · https://unjobs.org/vacancies/1746389103136)
3. **Written tests are common and timed.** The UN uses its own online test platform (UNOETS), video interviews, work samples and assessment centres (https://careers.un.org/assessment). EU Delegations put the final candidates through a written test and an interview (https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lebanon/project-officer-cooperation-section_en?s=203).
4. **Each employer scores against a named framework.** A client should learn the right one: UN Values and Behaviours (https://hr.un.org/en/un-values-and-behaviours-framework), UK Civil Service Success Profiles (https://www.civil-service-careers.gov.uk/a-guide-to-civil-service-success-profiles/), the IRC Success Model (https://careers.rescue.org/en/faq/faq.html), and EPSO's competency framework (https://eu-careers.europa.eu/en/selection-procedure/epso-tests).
5. **Lebanese nationals are shut out of most EU civil-service exams.** The permanent EPSO graduate exam is for EU nationals only (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/apply-graduate-competition-ad-5). For them the realistic EU route is a **Local Agent** post in the EU Delegation (https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lebanon/project-officer-cooperation-section_en?s=203).
6. **Safeguarding checks are now standard in INGOs.** More than 380 organisations use the Misconduct Disclosure Scheme. More than 220,000 checks have led to 677 applications being rejected at the recruitment stage (https://misconduct-disclosure-scheme.org/). Candidates should have referees and a police certificate ready.
7. **Competition is fierce in 2026.** The UN's proposed 2026 budget would cut 2,681 posts, or 18.8% (https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/12/1166480). Nearly all those cuts hit posts left empty by a hiring freeze (https://ishr.ch/latest-updates/un80-reform-states-endorse-slashing-of-human-rights-budget/). The 2026 EU graduate exam drew 174,727 applicants for 1,490 places, about 117 applicants per place (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/job-opportunities/graduate-administrators · https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/Notice-of-open-competition-EPSO-AD-427-26_consolidated-version.pdf).
8. **Rules on AI use are now explicit.** UNDP lets candidates use AI to proofread their applications and to practise interviews. It forbids AI during live interviews and in any test where AI is not explicitly allowed (https://www.undp.org/careers/using-ai-responsibly-undp-recruitment-and-selection). Kristin's prep should teach these rules.
9. **For Lebanon, Daleel Madani is the main job source.** It listed 169 jobs (https://daleel-madani.org/jobs, page cached 3 Oct 2026). A "Lebanon" search on ReliefWeb returned only 31 results (https://reliefweb.int/jobs?search=Lebanon, 4 Oct 2026).
10. **Automating the job tracker is only partly possible.** ReliefWeb has a free API, but it needs a pre-approved app name. A live call without one was refused (https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/). Daleel Madani sits behind a Cloudflare bot check (tested 4 Oct 2026). UN Careers publishes an RSS feed (https://careers.un.org/jobfeed?isPage=true).

## 2. The common pattern

| Stage | What happens | Where this is documented |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Screening | The application is checked against the minimum requirements. Many systems ask pre-screening questions. | UNDP: https://www.undp.org/careers/our-recruitment-process · NRC: https://www.nrc.no/career/five-steps-of-nrc-recruitment-process |
| 2. Assessment | A written test, technical test, reasoning test, pre-recorded video, work sample, presentation or assessment centre. | UN: https://careers.un.org/assessment · UNICEF: https://www.unicef.org/careers/get-prepared-assessment |
| 3. Interview | A panel interview, mostly competency-based, sometimes with technical questions. | UN: https://hr.un.org/en/cbi-training-applicants · UNDP: https://www.undp.org/careers/our-recruitment-process |

## 3. UN system

### 3.1 Where people apply
- **UN Secretariat:** jobs are on careers.un.org, and people apply through **Inspira** (https://careers.un.org/how-to-apply). Each job opening has "Competencies" and "Assessment" sections. One example P-3 opening was open for 14 days, from 22 Sep to 6 Oct 2026 (https://careers.un.org/jobSearchDescription/285249).
- **UN agencies use their own portals.** UNDP posts all its jobs on its own job page (https://www.undp.org/careers/our-recruitment-process). UNICEF has its own careers site (https://www.unicef.org/careers/get-prepared). UN Volunteers use the Unified Volunteer Platform, where you must first build a profile in the UNV Global Talent Pool (https://www.unv.org/become-volunteer).

### 3.2 Tests the UN uses
The UN Careers site lists these assessment formats (https://careers.un.org/assessment):
- a written test on the **UN Online Examinations and Tests System (UNOETS)**
- an **asynchronous (pre-recorded) video interview**
- **work samples** (for example a portfolio)
- an **assessment centre**

These tests can measure (same source): cognitive ability, situational judgement, personality, integrity, subject knowledge, communication or drafting skills, and language. Grading is anonymous, except for interviews and presentations (same source).

**UNDP** runs two phases (https://www.undp.org/careers/our-recruitment-process):
- Phase 1: a pre-recorded video interview and/or a written test (motivation, technical skills, language).
- Phase 2: a mainly competency-based interview, which may include technical questions and extra tests.
- Candidates who are not already UN staff then have a reference check.

**UNICEF** lists these techniques (https://www.unicef.org/careers/get-prepared-assessment): blended interview, technical interview, CBI, presentations, timed tests and exercises (writing, analysis, strategic thinking), assessment centres (more often for senior roles), desk review, informal conversation, and technical assessments (verbal, numerical and logical, plus online psychometric tests).

### 3.3 The competency-based interview
- **The principle:** past behaviour predicts future behaviour. Candidates should match their own past examples to the competencies named in the job opening (https://hr.un.org/en/cbi-training-applicants).
- **Answer structure:** "Situation, Action, Result" (same source).
- **Sample UN questions** (same source):
  - "Tell us about a challenging assignment when you went above and beyond your manager's expectations."
  - "Give an example of a time when you used your problem solving abilities to resolve an issue between staff members."
  - "How have you handled situations when you had a number of demands being made for delivery at the same time?"
- **Official prep material:** the UN Office at Geneva's "Career Tips Thursdays" webinars and worksheets (https://hr.un.org/en/cbi-training-applicants) and UNDP's 2025 CBI short guide (https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/cbi-shortguide-to-applicants-2025.pdf).
- **Framework:** the **UN Values and Behaviours Framework**. It has 4 values (Inclusion, Integrity, Humility, Humanity) and 5 behaviours (Connect and Collaborate; Analyse and Plan; Deliver Results with Positive Impact; Learn and Develop; Adapt and Innovate). It is meant to inform recruitment (https://hr.un.org/en/un-values-and-behaviours-framework). It builds on the 1999 "Competencies for the Future" (same source). Some job postings may still use the older competency list **(secondary source: https://www.unjobnet.org/career-resources/common-un-interview-questions-and-answers)**.

### 3.4 Rosters
Qualified candidates who are not selected can be put on a roster and offered later jobs in the same job family and grade without a new competition. The US State Department's guide says a roster place lasts 2 years for men and 3 years for women (https://iocareers.state.gov/Content/Public/process). **(Not checked against the UN's own rules.)**

### 3.5 Young Professionals Programme (YPP), for new grads
- An entrance exam held once a year. It is open to nationals of the countries taking part that year, and the list changes yearly (https://careers.un.org/young-professionals-programme).
- Applications usually open in June, through Inspira (same source).
- To apply you must be 32 or younger in the exam year, hold at least a first university degree in the field, and be fluent in English or French (https://learning.unog.ch/node/1482).
- The written exam has a **General Paper** (the same for all exam areas, in English or French) and a **Specialized Paper** (multiple-choice and open questions on the subject). People who pass take a competency-based video interview (https://www.un.org/en/academic-impact/work4un-young-professionals-programme).
- Other agencies run their own YPPs. For example, FAO's YPP closes 14 Oct 2026 **(secondary source: https://www.unjobnet.org/)**.

### 3.6 The 2026 context
- UN News (1 Dec 2025): member states owe nearly $1.6 billion in unpaid dues. The proposed 2026 cuts would remove 2,681 posts, an 18.8% reduction (https://news.un.org/en/story/2025/12/1166480).
- ISHR (9 Jan 2026): "Nearly all the cuts in the 2026 budget are to posts that were vacant as a result of a hiring freeze" (https://ishr.ch/latest-updates/un80-reform-states-endorse-slashing-of-human-rights-budget/).
- Global Policy Forum (10 Feb 2026): a 15% cut in the Secretariat budget would mean about 2,600 fewer staff (https://www.globalpolicy.org/en/news/2026-02-10/how-un-funding-crisis-will-worsen-2026).
- **What this means:** there are fewer UN openings and more experienced applicants for each one. Strong written-test and CBI performance matters more than ever, and so do rosters.

### 3.7 AI use (UNDP's written rules)
From https://www.undp.org/careers/using-ai-responsibly-undp-recruitment-and-selection:
- **Allowed:** proofreading and structuring; analysing job descriptions; practising with AI-generated interview questions; researching UNDP.
- **Not allowed:** making up or exaggerating experience; pasting generic AI answers without adapting them to your own experience; using AI during live interviews; using AI in any assessment where it is not explicitly allowed.

## 4. EU institutions and EU Delegations

### 4.1 Permanent EU civil service (EPSO competitions)
- **Graduate administrators (AD5).** Only EU nationals can apply. You need one EU language at a thorough level and a second at a satisfactory level, plus a degree of at least 3 years. No work experience is needed. The 2026 window was 5 Feb to 10 Mar 2026 (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/apply-graduate-competition-ad-5).
- **AD5 tests** (https://eu-careers.europa.eu/en/selection-procedure/epso-tests → Graduates page):
  - reasoning tests (verbal, numerical and abstract; multiple choice)
  - an EU knowledge test (multiple choice on EU institutions, procedures and policies)
  - a digital skills test (5 areas: information and data literacy, communication and collaboration, digital content creation, safety, problem-solving)
  - the **Free-Text Essay on EU Matters (EUFTE)**. It tests written communication, not facts. It is scored on 5 "anchors": logical structure, concision, clarity, fit to audience and purpose, and good use of the material provided.
- **Volume:** 174,727 applicants (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/job-opportunities/graduate-administrators; EPSO news, 8 May 2026: https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/news/ad5-graduates-final-number-candidates/19906). The notice of competition seeks **1,490** successful candidates (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/Notice-of-open-competition-EPSO-AD-427-26_consolidated-version.pdf, section 1(d)). That is about 117 applicants per place. Testing is set for November 2026 (indicative, same competition page). *(Corrected 4 Oct 2026: the earlier draft said "more than 170,000" from a LinkedIn snippet and "about 1,495" from a secondary site.)*
- **Passing is not a job offer.** Successful candidates go on a reserve list. Institutions then invite them to recruitment interviews, usually in English or French (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/apply-graduate-competition-ad-5).
- EPSO does not offer or endorse any prep courses (https://eu-careers.europa.eu/en/selection-procedure/epso-tests).

### 4.2 EU contract staff (CAST)
- Registration is always open, with no deadline. There are 4 function groups (FG I–IV). FG IV needs at least 3 years of university. You need C1 in one EU language and B2 in another (https://selection.eu-careers.europa.eu/en/contract-staff-selection-procedures-always-open-registration).
- When an institution needs someone, it shortlists from the database. Shortlisted people take verbal, numerical and abstract reasoning tests. **Results stay valid for 10 years.** Upcoming test dates: 6 Oct 2026 and 19 Nov 2026 (same source).
- At EEAS headquarters, contract-agent posts are for EU nationals. Contract-agent posts in Delegations need a CAST pass in the right function group (https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/general-conditions-contract-agent-posts_en).

### 4.3 EU Delegation Local Agents (the most relevant route for clients in Lebanon)
Example: Project Officer, Cooperation Section, EU Delegation to Lebanon, Local Agent Group 1, posted 2024 (https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lebanon/project-officer-cooperation-section_en?s=203):
- **Application:** a cover letter, a **Europass-format CV** and a declaration on honour, sent by email.
- **Minimum requirements:** a BA and 3 years of experience in cooperation projects.
- **Selection criteria** are listed separately and earn points (for example project management, negotiation and advocacy, contract and financial management, English and Arabic, drafting skills, knowledge of the EU in Lebanon).
- **Process:** shortlisting, then written testing and interviews. "At least three best candidates will be invited to the final interview and written test."
- **Checks:** medical, background, and verification of diplomas and language certificates.
- The Delegation does not discuss the selection procedure.
- **What this means:** clients need a Europass CV, a cover letter that answers each selection criterion, timed drafting practice in English, and knowledge of what the EU does in Lebanon.

## 5. Embassies and foreign ministries (local hires)

### 5.1 United States embassies
- Applications go through the **Electronic Recruitment Application (ERA)** (https://erajobs.state.gov/dos-era/vacancysearch/searchVacancies.hms, and https://nz.usembassy.gov/job-opportunities/era-the-application-process/).
- **Process** (US Embassy Uzbekistan: https://uz.usembassy.gov/interview-and-pre-employment-clearances/):
  - Finalists are invited to an interview and/or an assessment.
  - Some posts need a job-related test. Language posts can include speaking, reading and writing tests.
  - The panel is usually a US representative from the hiring office, a subject-matter person and HR.
  - References are checked.
  - A tentative offer comes first, then a medical exam and a security investigation.
  - Do not resign from your current job until the formal offer arrives.
- **Details from US Mission Switzerland** (https://ch.usembassy.gov/understanding-the-application-process/):
  - Your CV or DS-174 form must cover **every** requirement, or it will not be considered.
  - Leave race, religion, marital status and similar details off the CV.
  - Give 3 personal and 3 professional references.
  - Shortlisting and interviews usually finish within 6 weeks of the closing date.
  - There is no feedback after an unsuccessful interview.
  - Eligible family members of US staff get hiring preference.

### 5.2 United Kingdom (FCDO, which is both an embassy and a donor)
- In Lebanon, jobs at the British Embassy Beirut and other UK offices are posted on the FCDO local staff site (https://www.gov.uk/world/organisations/british-embassy-beirut/about/recruitment).
- Example FCDO country-based post, Jakarta, 2025 (https://unjobs.org/vacancies/1746389103136):
  - The application form asks **behaviour-based questions**. This post asked for "Communicating and Influencing" and "Seeing the Big Picture."
  - Candidates are told to read the Success Profiles behaviours and use **STAR**.
  - CVs must be **anonymised**: no name, address, email, age, date of birth, gender or nationality.
  - Strong runners-up can go on a **6-month reserve list**.
  - Offers depend on security vetting. Candidates arrange their own police certificate.
  - Applications are only accepted through the official tal.net site.
- **Success Profiles** (https://www.civil-service-careers.gov.uk/a-guide-to-civil-service-success-profiles/) has 5 elements: Behaviours, Strengths, Ability, Experience and Technical. Possible assessments include the Civil Service judgement test, personality tests, verbal and numerical reasoning tests, presentations, in-tray exercises, written analysis, role-play, oral briefings and assessment centres. For **strengths questions** the advice is "Do not rehearse your answers." Candidates should reflect on what energises them instead.

### 5.3 Australia
- Example LES vacancy at the Australian Embassy Beirut: a CV, two referees, and a **500–700 word statement** that answers the selection criteria with concrete examples. Arabic was essential. Security vetting applied (https://lebanon.embassy.gov.au/files/birt/Consular%20Passport%20Officer%20and%20Property%20Support.pdf). **This ad dates from 2017, so the current format may differ.**

### 5.4 Sweden (Sida)
- Jobs at Sida in Sweden need Swedish. Each embassy recruits its own local staff, and posts them on Sweden Abroad. "The recruitment process and criteria differ depending on the country and the position" (https://sida.se/en/about-sida/jobs-at-sida).

### 5.5 Common points across embassies
- You must already have the right to work locally. Embassies do not sponsor permits (https://ch.usembassy.gov/understanding-the-application-process/ · https://unjobs.org/vacancies/1746389103136).
- Language tests and security vetting are normal.
- Other embassies (France, Germany, the Netherlands and others) were **not researched** for this file.

## 6. Major INGOs

### 6.1 Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC)
From https://www.nrc.no/career/five-steps-of-nrc-recruitment-process:
1. **Screening.** Languages are checked first, then experience. Expect an answer within about one month. No answer means you were not selected.
2. **Tests,** chosen to fit the role: aptitude (numerical, verbal, abstract), personality, technical tests (for example education, security, finance), and language tests, often during the interview.
3. **Usually two interviews,** with HR, a technical specialist and the hiring manager. They cover technical and behavioural competencies. Field candidates are also asked about stress, resilience and health.
4. **Background checks,** including references. Some roles need police or child-protection record checks.
5. **Offer.** International field roles need medical clearance.

The whole process can take up to four months. Only interviewed candidates get feedback (https://www.nrc.no/career/frequently-asked-questions).

NRC's expert roster (NORCAP) uses a video interview, a personality assessment, technical tests, then a **job simulation** and a structured competency interview (https://www.nrc.no/career/five-steps-of-norcap-recruitment-process).

### 6.2 International Rescue Committee (IRC)
From https://careers.rescue.org/en/faq/faq.html:
- **Three rounds are typical:** the recruiter, then the hiring manager, then a deeper role-specific session. The later rounds can be panels.
- References are checked before the offer. A background check follows the offer.
- **What they look for:** the IRC Success Model (Receptive, Resourceful, Resilient, Deliver Results) and "The IRC Way" values (Integrity, Service, Accountability, Equality).
- **Scam warning:** the IRC never charges fees and never asks for passport numbers during interviews.

### 6.3 Save the Children
- Save the Children Australia: most processes have 2 interviews, sometimes a 3rd or psychometric testing. Pre-employment checks include a police check, the Misconduct Disclosure Scheme, 2 professional referees, and signing the Child Safeguarding Policy (https://www.savethechildren.org.au/about-us/careers/our-application-process).
- Field offices often add a written test **(secondary source, crowd-sourced: https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Save-the-Children-International-Interview-Questions-E495188.htm)**.

### 6.4 Sector-wide tools
- **Core Humanitarian Competency Framework (CHCF):** a shared competency framework for the humanitarian sector, revised in 2017. It is available in English, **Arabic**, French and other languages, with a guide for HR staff on competency-based recruitment (https://www.chsalliance.org/get-support/resource/core-humanitarian-competency-framework/).
- **Misconduct Disclosure Scheme:** used by more than 380 organisations, with more than 220,000 checks and 677 applications rejected at the recruitment stage (https://misconduct-disclosure-scheme.org/). The scheme covers sexual misconduct (same source). Candidates must give consent before reference checks start (https://misconduct-disclosure-scheme.org/faqs).

### 6.5 Common INGO written tests
In practice, field written tests often ask candidates to draft a short concept note, a logframe, an M&E plan or a budget narrative, or to analyse a scenario within 1–3 hours. **(Unverified. Taken from general sector knowledge, not from a primary source in this research. Check against real test invitations Kristin's clients receive.)**

## 8. CSR and corporate sustainability roles

These processes are less standardised. Little was confirmed from primary sources.
- Expect normal corporate hiring: an applicant tracking system, a recruiter screen, a hiring-manager interview and a panel. Sustainability and ESG roles often add a **case study or presentation** (for example an emissions scope 1/2/3 problem, a materiality assessment, or a CSR strategy pitch). **(Secondary sources: https://enable.green/news/10-interview-tips-impress-esg-recruiters/ · https://csrjobs.club/blog/how-to-prepare-for-case-study-interviews-in-sustainability-consulting · https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/pages/sustainability-case-interview)**
- **Credentials that help with positioning:** the GRI Certified Sustainability Professional exam has 40 questions in 60 minutes, with a 75% pass mark (https://www.globalreporting.org/academy/certification/). This suits Kristin's "profile gap → certificates" service.
- In MENA, corporate CSR roles are more likely to appear on Bayt and LinkedIn than on humanitarian boards **(unverified)**.

## Limitations

- **Point in time.** Prices, job counts and test formats were checked on 4 October 2026. Job counts change daily.
- **Collapsed sections.** The UN Careers pages for YPP exam structure and a job's "Assessment" section load as collapsed menus. The scraper could not open them. YPP details come from other official UN pages (UNOG, UN Academic Impact).
- **Search snippets only.** The Impactpool €560 coaching price and the Glassdoor timing figures were seen only in search snippets. The pages themselves were not opened. (The EPSO applicant figure was confirmed on EPSO's own page during the 4 Oct fact-check.)
- **Unverified details.** The ReliefWeb API rate limits came from an automated extraction and are not confirmed. The UN roster durations come from a US government guide, not from UN rules.
- **Single examples.** The Australian Embassy example dates from 2017. The FCDO example is from Jakarta, not Beirut. The EU Delegation example is from 2024. Formats in Beirut today may differ.
- **Not covered.** Hiring at French, German, Dutch, Canadian and Gulf-state embassies, at ICRC/IFRC, MSF, Oxfam and World Bank/IFIs, and at Lebanese ministries was not researched. Bond, CharityJob and EuroBrussels were not checked.
- **CSR is thin.** CSR hiring has no shared standard. Most CSR claims here rest on secondary sources.
- **INGO test formats.** The typical INGO written-test tasks (concept note, logframe and so on) are general sector knowledge and are marked unverified. Kristin should collect real test invitations from her clients to build a prep bank.
- **Automation tests were simple.** The checks of Daleel Madani, UN Careers RSS and the ReliefWeb API used plain scripted requests. A browser-based tool or an approved API key may succeed where these failed.
