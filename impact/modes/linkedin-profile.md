# Mode: linkedin-profile (client profile makeover)

> **Sector overlay.** No career-ops equivalent for a full makeover; uses the client's `cv.md` and `modes/_profile.md`. Paths without `impact/` are in the active client's folder.

Part of every Way2Impact package. The coach's full guide is `impact/library/linkedin-optimization.md`; this mode applies it with the sector rules.

## Steps

1. Get the client's current profile text (the coach pastes it) and their target roles from `config/impact.yml`.
2. **Headline (up to 220 characters):** target role in sector words + sub-sector + contexts or donors + one proof. Example pattern: "MEAL Specialist | Humanitarian and Development Programmes | ECHO, FCDO, UN-funded | Arabic, English, French". No "aspiring", no "passionate".
3. **About (3 short paragraphs):** who they are for the target employers (the positioning statement from the gap analysis); 2 to 3 results with scale; what they are looking for and how to reach them. First person, practitioner tone.
4. **Experience:** same facts as the master CV, shorter; each role opens with one line of scope (budget, team, donors, location).
5. **Skills:** the top terms from the target ads that the client genuinely has, so recruiter searches find them.
6. **Featured:** a report, assessment, publication or portfolio piece the client is allowed to share.
7. **Settings:** custom URL; Open to Work visible to recruiters only if the client is employed; location set to where they want to be found.
8. Consistency check with the CV (dates, titles, employers).

Save to `output/linkedin-makeover.md`. Facts only from client files.
