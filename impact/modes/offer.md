# Mode: offer (what can and cannot be negotiated)

> **Sector overlay.** Runs on top of career-ops `modes/offer-prep.md`. Paths without `impact/` are in the active client's folder.

Not legal advice. Uses `impact/sector/research/05` §5.

## UN system

- The **grade is fixed** by the classified post. Do not ask for a higher grade or "more salary".
- Ask about the **step**: new appointees start at Step I by default; each full year of relevant experience above the minimum can add a step, up to a ceiling (Secretariat P-1 to P-5: Step VII). Script: "Could you tell me how my step placement was determined, given my experience beyond the minimum?"
- Compare offers on **net pay at the duty station** (base salary plus post adjustment, which varies by location), not on base salary.
- Negotiable or worth clarifying: **start date**, relocation and shipment, rental subsidy eligibility, education grant where relevant, contract length.
- Know the contract: FTA (usually 2 years, renewable, full benefits, no expectancy of renewal), TA (under 1 year), consultancy (not staff, no pension).

## INGOs and NGOs

- Ask for the **salary scale and the placement criteria**. Research comparable posts and cost of living.
- Hardball rarely works with strict bands; ask whether there is room and on what criteria.
- National and international scales usually differ; a national hire rarely moves to an expat package.
- Negotiate non-salary items: leave, flexible or remote days, learning budget, start date, R&R for hardship posts.

## Consultancies

Quote within the published band; clarify daily rate vs lump sum; for contractors, leverage is highest when the budget is being built (proposal stage).

## Segment C

Expect lower pay than corporate. If asked, answer calmly with a range based on the sector scale, not the old corporate salary.

## Output

A one-page summary for the client: what is fixed, what to ask, exact scripts, and the questions to send HR in writing. Save to `output/NNN-offer.md`.
