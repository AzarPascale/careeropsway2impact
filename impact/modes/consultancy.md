# Mode: consultancy (calls for consultants, ToRs, EOIs, RFPs)

> **Sector overlay.** Uses career-ops `modes/oferta.md` for the evaluation report; this file adds the consultancy-specific parts. Paths without `impact/` are in the active client's folder.

Consultancies are a growing share of the market after the cuts and a key bridge for segments C and D. Uses `impact/sector/research/05` §5.1, §5.3, §7.

## 1. Evaluate the call (short version of `impact/modes/evaluate.md`)

- **Who can apply:** individual or firm? Nationality or registration rules? Required degree and years?
- **Scope:** deliverables, number of days, dates, location (home-based, field days).
- **Selection method:** lowest price, quality and cost (for example 70/30), or quality only. This decides how much effort the technical part deserves.
- **Fee:** daily rate or lump sum? UN Secretariat consultant bands run from about USD 190 a day (level A) to about USD 1,000 to 1,100 (level E) (OHR/PG/2025/2, snippet); UNDP raised its bands in September 2025. Quote within the band for the level.
- **Contract reality:** consultants are not staff; usually no pension or staff leave; a cap of 24 months in 36 is reported at UNDP. Do not present a consultancy as a route into a staff post.
- **Proposal recruitment:** if a contractor asks the client to be "key personnel" for a bid, it is a job only if the bid wins.
- Score with `impact/config/scoring.yml`, contract weight scored to the client's preference.

## 2. What to draft

| Document | Content |
|---|---|
| Expression of interest (1 to 2 pages) | Understanding of the assignment in the client's words; 3 most relevant past assignments with client, dates, result; availability; daily rate if asked |
| Technical proposal or methodology note | Understanding of context and ToR; approach and methods (for evaluations: OECD DAC criteria, sampling, tools, ethics, data protection); work plan by deliverable; risks and mitigation; team |
| CV annex | Topical or functional by expertise area, or the donor's template; list of assignments with dates and clients |
| Financial proposal | Days per deliverable x rate; travel and other costs as the call asks |

## 3. Rules

- Mirror the ToR's structure and words.
- Every past assignment named must be in the client's files. No borrowed references.
- Respect page limits; evaluators score against a grid, so make each criterion easy to find (headings that match the grid).
- Save to `output/NNN-eoi.md` (and `-methodology.md`, `-cv-annex.md`).
