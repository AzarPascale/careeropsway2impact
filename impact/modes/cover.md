# Mode: cover (cover letter, motivation letter, supporting statement)

> **Sector overlay.** Runs on top of career-ops `modes/cover.md` (`generate-cover-letter.mjs` makes the PDF). Paths without `impact/` are in the active client's folder.

Uses `impact/library/cover-letter/framework.md` (the coach's method) and the segment examples in `impact/library/cover-letter/segment-*.md`, plus `impact/sector/language-guide.md`.

## 1. Choose the document

| The ad or employer asks for | Write |
|---|---|
| Cover letter (INGO, NGO, foundation, CSR) | One page or less |
| UN motivation statement | Use `impact/modes/un-form.md` §4 |
| UK charity "supporting statement" or "personal statement" against a person specification | One heading per essential criterion, evidence under each; up to 2 sides of A4 if allowed |
| Questions in the application form | Answer each directly: one point, one example |
| Email application | Short letter in the email body, CV attached |

Check the employer's **AI rule** first (some UK charities advise against AI-written statements; UNDP allows proofreading). Tell the coach before drafting if there is a restriction.

## 2. Structure of a one-page letter (default)

1. **Opening (2 to 3 lines):** the role, and why this organisation's mandate matters to the client **and why now**. Name one specific programme, place, strategy or value, linked to one real experience. No "I am writing to apply".
2. **Three short paragraphs:** each takes one key requirement from the evaluation's Block B and proves it with one specific example from the client's files (action, scale, result). Do not repeat the CV line by line; add the how and why.
3. **Context paragraph (optional):** the client's knowledge of the country, partners, the funding situation, or localisation and partnership approach, where relevant.
4. **Close (2 lines):** what the client brings to the team now, availability, thanks.

## 3. Segment angle

- A: link studies, internships or volunteering to the programme; energy shown through evidence.
- B: scale and strategic contribution; why the next level now.
- C: why this move, what transfers, humility about what they will learn; never "giving back" or a favour to the sector.
- D: one calm line on the role ending if useful; focus on what they bring.

## 4. Mission alignment done right

Do: name one concrete thing (a programme, a report, a strategy, a partnership approach) and connect it to one real experience.
Don't: "make a difference", "give back", "passionate", "help those in need", or a life story.

## 5. Checks

Run `impact/modes/_shared.md` §4. Add: the letter sounds like a practitioner, not a template; every paragraph maps to a requirement; length and limits respected. Save to `output/NNN-letter.md`.
