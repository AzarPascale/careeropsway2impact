# Mode: interview (competency-based interview prep)

> **Sector overlay.** Runs on top of career-ops `modes/interview-prep.md` and `modes/interview/*` (practice and debrief). Paths without `impact/` are in the active client's folder.

Uses `impact/library/interview-and-tests/un-competency-based-interviews.md`, `question-bank.md`, `mock-interview-protocol.md`, `embassy-and-donor-processes.md`, and the evaluation report's Block F.

## 1. Build the brief (save as `output/NNN-interview-prep.md`)

1. **Format:** who sits on the panel (usually HR, a technical expert, the hiring manager), length, language checks, whether a test or presentation comes first.
2. **Framework:** name the competency framework (UN values and behaviours, the agency's own, CHS Core Humanitarian Competency Framework, the INGO's). List the competencies in the job opening.
3. **Context brief (one page):** the organisation's mandate and programme in the country, recent news (cuts, mergers, new strategy), main donors and partners, the crisis or development context. Facts with sources.
4. **Likely questions:** 10 to 15, mapped to the competencies. Include:
   - "Tell us about yourself" (90 seconds, tailored to the hiring problem),
   - "Why this organisation and why this role?",
   - a mistake or failure question,
   - values and **safeguarding** questions (how you would make the organisation safer; how you would respond to a concern),
   - for field roles: stress, security, working in a team under pressure,
   - for segment D: the gap and the role ending,
   - for segment C: why the sector now, and pay expectations.
5. **Story bank:** 8 to 10 real stories from the last 5 to 7 years, each in **STAR + learning** form: Situation 2 to 3 sentences; Task = the client's own responsibility; **Action about 60% of the answer, in "I"**; Result with numbers plus what they learned or changed. 2 to 3 minutes each. Use `interview-prep/story-bank.md`; mark new stories needed. Facts only from client files or coach confirmation. At P-4 and above, panels look for strategic impact, leadership of teams and partnerships, judgement under uncertainty, and results at scale.
   - Technical questions are often scenarios ("a donor project is behind schedule", "an audit finds unsupported costs"): they test approach, not definitions. Include stakeholder and donor communication in judgement answers.
6. **Questions to ask the panel:** 3 to 5 (team and partners, funding horizon of the post, how success is measured in the first 6 months, how the team works with local actors).

## 2. Sector norms to coach

- **One real past example per answer,** not hypotheticals or "we always".
- **Humility plus ownership:** credit the team, then say exactly what the client did ("I", not "we").
- **Context knowledge** beats generic answers about "the crisis".
- **Localisation and power awareness** where relevant: working with or under local leadership.
- Prepare a mistake story with real learning.
- Never use AI or notes on a second screen during the interview (panels notice, and it breaks trust).
- After the interview: thank-you email within 24 hours (draft only), and a debrief in the tracker.

## 3. Practice

If the coach asks for practice: play the panel, one question at a time, wait for the answer, then give feedback on structure (STAR), evidence (numbers, own role), competency fit and length. Follow `impact/library/interview-and-tests/mock-interview-protocol.md`.
