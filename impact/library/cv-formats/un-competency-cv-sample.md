# Sample competency-based CV (UN and large INGO)

**Source of the model:** the sample CVs Orestes Sotta (former Head of HR, WFP Tanzania) posts on LinkedIn for live UN vacancies (UNICEF P-4 Procurement, Chief of HR P-5, Finance and Budget Officer P-3, WHO Representative roster, WFP Executive Director). His section order, competency split and bullet patterns are summarised in `impact/content/research/profiles-batch-a.md` §1. **None of his text is copied.** The candidate below is fictional, built the same way he builds his: one hypothetical person written to one type of vacancy.

Use this file when `impact/modes/cv.md` needs a CV (not form text) for a UN agency or a large INGO with a named competency framework. For Inspira and P11 form text, use `impact/modes/un-form.md`.

---

## 1. The section order

1. **Header:** name, a title line matched to the target role, city and country, phone, email, LinkedIn. Nationality on UN CVs (it matters for eligibility and geographic balance).
2. **Professional summary** (4 to 5 sentences): years in the function and sector, years inside the UN or INGO system, current grade and scope, 2 to 3 headline results with numbers, highest degree, languages with levels.
3. **Core competencies,** in two blocks:
   - **Technical:** keywords from the vacancy, only where true.
   - **Leadership:** phrased in the agency's own framework (UN Values and Behaviours, UNICEF, WHO, or the INGO's framework).
4. **Professional experience,** reverse order. Each role: `Title (Grade), Organisation, City, Country` and `Month Year to Month Year`. Current role 6 to 8 bullets, older roles 3 to 5, oldest 1 to 3.
5. **Education:** degree, institution, year.
6. **Certifications** with year.
7. **Languages** with honest levels (UN: fluent, confident, working knowledge, or the agency's own scale).
8. **Technical skills:** named systems (Kobo, Power BI, SAP, Salesforce...), not "databases".

Length: 2 pages up to P-3, 2 to 3 pages for P-4 and P-5. D-level and above: condense to about 4 roles and lead with scale.

## 2. Bullet patterns (every bullet carries a number)

- Manage a portfolio of [N] projects worth USD [X]M with [N] partners in [N] countries, including [fragile or humanitarian] contexts.
- Redesigned [system, framework, process], cutting [metric] from [A] to [B] in [timeframe].
- Led a USD [X]M multi-partner [programme] with [ministries, donors, partners] across [N] countries, delivered [on time, under budget].
- Built [dashboard, monitoring system], raising [indicator] from [A]% to [B]%.
- Advise [Representative, senior management] on [topic], contributing to [measurable change].
- Trained [N] [staff, partner staff] on [system, standard].
- Lead and develop a team of [N] [function] staff.

Then run the three-question test from `impact/modes/cv.md` §3 on each bullet.

## 4. Before handing over

- Minimum requirements visible in the summary: degree level, years, required languages, mandatory certificates.
- Portal profile (Inspira, Workday, Taleo) matches the CV: same titles, dates and employers.
- Plain-text test passed (`impact/modes/cv.md` §3, step 8).
- Every fact comes from the client's files. The example above is a pattern, never content to reuse.
