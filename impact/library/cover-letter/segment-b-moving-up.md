**Client-facing.** This page speaks to you, the client, as "you". You can read it in Week 2 of the programme.

## The fictional advert (short version)

**Partnerships Specialist**, [ORGANISATION-B], regional office, [CITY-B]. Ref. [JOB-REF-B].

[ORGANISATION-B] is a fictional UN agency. The job opening asks for a CV and a one-page motivation letter.

Requirements:
1. A master's degree in international relations, development, business or a related field.
2. At least 5 years of experience in development or humanitarian work, including partnerships or resource mobilisation.
3. Experience with non-traditional donors, such as Gulf donors or development banks.
4. Strong proposal writing and donor reporting.
5. Fluent English and Arabic.

Competencies named in the opening (from the UN Values and Behaviours Framework): Connect and Collaborate; Deliver Results with Positive Impact; Adapt and Innovate.

## The letter

> [CANDIDATE-B-NAME]
> [CANDIDATE-B-CITY] · [CANDIDATE-B-EMAIL] · [CANDIDATE-B-PHONE]
>
> [DATE]
>
> **Subject: Partnerships Specialist, [ORGANISATION-B] regional office (Ref. [JOB-REF-B])**
>
> Dear Hiring Manager,
>
> [ORGANISATION-B] is widening its funding base in the region at a time when traditional funding is shrinking. That is the work I have been doing for the past three years, and I want to do it at a regional scale, with a mandate that reaches more people than one country programme can.
>
> As Grants Coordinator at [INGO-B], I lead proposals and donor relations for our country office. The Partnerships Specialist role is the next step: from winning grants for one office to building partnerships for a region.
>
> My experience matches the role and its competencies:
>
> - **Deliver Results with Positive Impact.** In 2025, two of our largest grants ended early. I led a team of four to rebuild the pipeline. In nine months we secured three new grants worth USD 1.8 million, and kept 80% of our education staff in post.
> - **Non-traditional donors.** I opened our first relationship with a Gulf-based foundation. I prepared the concept note in Arabic and English, and the foundation funded a two-year pilot.
> - **Connect and Collaborate.** I co-designed a joint proposal with two local partners and a development bank's country team. I chaired the weekly working group and kept all parties on one timeline.
> - **Adapt and Innovate.** I replaced our manual donor-reporting calendar with a shared tracker. Late reports fell from five in one year to none the next.
>
> I hold a master's in development studies and have seven years in the sector, the last three in partnerships. I work fully in Arabic and English.
>
> I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help [ORGANISATION-B] grow its partnerships in the region. I am available for interview at your convenience, and my notice period is [NOTICE-PERIOD].
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [CANDIDATE-B-NAME]

## Why this works

> **Bigger scope, said plainly.** Paragraph 2 names the step up in one line: from one office to a region. Moving up means showing you already work at the next level.
>
> **Proof mapped to the employer's own framework.** Three of the four examples are labelled with the competencies the opening names. UN panels score against these (research/03 §3.3), so the letter does the matching for them.
>
> **Revenue, partnerships, change.** For people moving up, the research points to money-raising, partnerships and leading through change (research/01 §8.2). Every example sits in that space.
>
> **Every requirement covered.** Degree, years, non-traditional donors, writing and languages each appear once, with evidence.
>
> **Results in numbers.** Funds raised, staff kept, late reports cut. The reader sees outcomes, not duties.

## Make it yours

- Use the competencies named in your job opening. Some postings may still use the older UN competency list (research/03 §3.3, secondary source). Use whichever one the opening shows.
- If the portal has a motivation field with a word limit, cut paragraph 3 to the two or three strongest examples.
- AI can help you proofread and structure. It must not invent or inflate experience (research/03 §3.7).
