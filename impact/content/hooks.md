# Hook library (templates)

Generalised templates distilled from the research in `impact/content/research/` (Mai Nazzal, Chahrazed Anane, Eunice Victoria, Nelly Kerubo, How To Find A Job, Lena Moral Waldmeier, saved posts). Fill the brackets with real facts only. Rotate; re-run winners every 6 to 8 weeks with a new hook.

## Data and market
1. "[Org or system] cut [N] jobs in [period]. We went through the official data, [grade by grade / region by region]."
2. "[X]% of this week's [N] roles are [remote / open to new grads / consultancies]."
3. "ReliefWeb postings are down [X]% from the peak. Here is where hiring is still happening."
4. "[Donor] just announced [X]. Here is what it means for your job search."

## Silence and rejection (validation, then steps)
5. "If you applied for a [UN / NGO] role in [month] and still have not heard back: it is [current month]. Here is what that silence means."
6. "[N] applications. [0] interviews. It is rarely your experience. It is usually the translation."
7. "Shortlisted, tested, interviewed. Then nothing. Read this before you apply again."

## Before and after
8. "Same person. Same [N] years. One CV got ignored. The other got shortlisted."
9. "This one line is costing [new grads / career switchers] interviews."
10. "'Responsible for...' Delete it. Here is what to write instead."
11. "The [summary / headline] mistake we see in most [MEAL / programme] CVs. It is not [X]. It is not [Y]. It is [Z]."

## Vacancy breakdown
12. "[Role] at [Org] is not just a [title]. It is [the real mix]."
13. "The job title is not the job. Look at this [Org] post in [City]."
14. "[Org] is hiring a [title], [grade], in [city]. Before you tailor anything, check these 5 things."

## Insider explainers
15. "Nobody tells you this about [UN rosters / Inspira / written tests / consultancy contracts]."
16. "Recruiter phrase → what it really means." (list)
17. "[UN / INGO] interviews are not [corporate] interviews. 5 differences."
18. "Why experienced [NGO] professionals struggle to move into the [UN]."
19. "Most people think there is one way into the UN. There are at least [N]."

## Segment-specific
20. New grads: "No paid sector experience yet? These [N] entry routes are still hiring."
21. Moving up: "One grade up, not two. How to read your real level in a [UN] ad."
22. Switchers: "Your corporate results are relevant. Your corporate words are not."
23. Repositioning: "Your role ended with the cuts. Your career did not. Where [your skill] is still needed."

## "If I were..." (numbered advice)
24. "If I were [a new grad / laid off from a UN agency / switching from finance] today, here is exactly what I would do in the next 30 days."

## Resource and CTA
25. "We rebuilt a [function] CV for the [sector]. Free template in the first comment."
26. "Save this before your next [UN application / panel interview]."

## Rules
- The hook must be true and backed in the body.
- Never borrow someone else's personal authority ("I sat on panels"). Way2Impact speaks as "we"; personal notes are attributed to Kristin.
- No clickbait the body does not pay off.
